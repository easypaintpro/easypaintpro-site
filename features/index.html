Drawing and painting

Freehand tools that feel like classic Paint, with sharper results. 12 features

  • Pencil

    Smooth freehand lines that stay sharp, and stay editable after you draw them.

    Toolbar ▸ Pencil

  • Pen weights and 10 pen styles

    One-click weights from 1 to 8 px. Styles: solid, dashed, dotted, textured, crayon, light, arrow at the start, the end or both, and shadow.

    Pencil ▸ Tool Options

  • Brush

    Paint with 22 classic tips or 29 professional brushes. Strokes you place stay editable.

    Toolbar ▸ Brush, or press B

  • Airbrush

    Hold to build up a soft spray. Go over an area again to make it denser.

    Toolbar ▸ Airbrush

  • Eraser

    Erases to your second color. The outline shows exactly what it will erase.

    Toolbar ▸ Eraser, or press E

  • Left and right mouse colors

    The left button paints with your main color and the right button with your second color, for drawing, fills, shapes and stamps, just like classic Paint.

    Any drawing tool

  • Size and opacity for each tool

    Every tool remembers its own size and opacity, even after you quit. Press [ and ] to change the size.

    Tool Options sliders

  • Color Picker

    Click any spot to take its color (right-click for the second color), then you’re back on the tool you were using.

    Toolbar ▸ Color Picker, or press I

  • Measure

    Drag between two points to read the distance in pixels, across and down. Nothing on the picture changes.

    Toolbar (scrolling tools) or More ▸ Measure

  • Pen pressure

    Professional brushes such as Pressure Size, Brush Pen and Pointed Pen follow the pressure of a drawing tablet pen.

    Brush ▸ Professional

  • Live mark preview

    See the exact size, opacity and color of your next mark before you draw.

    Tool Options

  • Tool-shaped pointer

    The pointer shows the tool, or the brush’s exact footprint. Prefer a plain dot? You can switch.

    View ▸ Canvas Cursor

  • Hardness and flow

    Make a professional brush softer or harder, lighter or heavier.

    Brush ▸ Tool Options

  • Textures that stay put

    Watercolor, crayon and grain textures look the same after you save and reopen.

    Automatic

  • Highlighter colors

    Six marker colors, yellow to purple, plus any color you like.

    Brush ▸ Highlighter

  • Brushes that explain themselves

    Point at a professional brush to read what it’s for.

    Hover over a brush

Fills, gradients, patterns and emoji

Fill an area or any shape four ways: solid, gradient, pattern or emoji. 7 features

  • Fill With Color

    Fill a connected area with a solid color, gradient, pattern or emoji. A similarity setting decides how far it spreads.

    Toolbar ▸ Fill, or press G

  • Five gradients

    Linear, radial, angle, reflected and diamond, blending your main color into your second color.

    Colors ▸ gradient square, or Fill style ▸ Gradient

  • 38 patterns, 1× to 8×

    Classic two-color tiles such as brick, basket, weave, herringbone and pebbles, with a size slider and a live preview.

    Fill style ▸ Pattern

  • Seamless patterns

    Patterns line up with the canvas, so two fills next to each other meet with no seam.

    Automatic

  • Emoji fill

    Fill any area or shape with a repeating emoji, from 16 to 128 px. 56 quick picks, or any emoji on your Mac.

    Fill style ▸ Emoji

  • Emoji stickers

    Double-click or drag an emoji to place it as an editable sticker.

    Emoji picker

  • Shape finish

    Outline only, fill only, or both.

    Shape tools ▸ Tool Options

Shapes and lines

30 ready-made shapes plus lines, curves and polygons that wait until you’re happy. 8 features

  • 30 shapes

    Stars, hearts, arrows, block arrows, chevrons, speech and thought bubbles, callouts, gear, lightning, check and X marks, polygons from triangle to octagon, and more.

    Toolbar ▸ Shapes, or press U

  • Line and Curve

    Straight lines (hold Shift for 45° steps), and curves you bend by dragging.

    Toolbar ▸ Line, Curve

  • Rectangle, ellipse, rounded rectangle, polygon

    Hold Shift for a perfect square or circle. Rounded corners from 2 to 40 px.

    Toolbar

  • Shapes wait for you

    Move, resize and restyle a new shape, then press Return or click outside to place it. That click starts the next one.

    All shape tools

  • Editable after placing

    Shapes, lines, strokes and text stay objects. Double-click one with Select/Move, or Command-click it with any tool.

    Select/Move, or press V

  • Rotate to any angle

    A round handle turns objects. Hold Shift to snap to 15°.

    Rotation handle

  • In front or behind

    Place something in front of your drawing, or behind it so it only shows on blank paper.

    Tool Options ▸ Place

  • Double-click or drag to add

    Double-click a shape, stamp or 3D tile to add it, or drag it onto the canvas.

    Shape, stamp and 3D galleries
