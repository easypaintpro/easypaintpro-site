Privacy tools

Share the picture, not the private details.

Blur, pixelate or black out names and numbers in a screenshot, then export a clean copy. No account, no upload.

Editable workspaces and recordings can keep the history of what you covered. Share the flattened export.

PDF pages

Mark up a PDF page like a picture.

Each PDF page opens as its own paint tab. Draw, stamp or add text, then save a new PDF.

Pages are imported as images, so the original PDF text and form fields aren't editable. For filling forms and reordering original pages, Preview is the better tool.
