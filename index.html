Privacy tools

Share the picture, not the private details.

Blur, pixelate or black out names and numbers in a screenshot, then export a clean copy. No account, no upload.

Editable workspaces and recordings can keep the history of what you covered. Share the flattened export.

PDF pages

Mark up a PDF page like a picture.

Each PDF page opens as its own paint tab. Draw, stamp or add text, then save a new PDF.

Pages are imported as images, so the original PDF text and form fields aren't editable. For filling forms and reordering original pages, Preview is the better tool.

Privacy

Easy Paint Pro is designed for local image editing. Your artwork is processed on your Mac. The app does not require an account for ordinary editing and does not upload your images to an Easy Paint Pro server.

What stays local

Images, canvas history, layers, masks, recordings, workspaces and exports stay on your device unless you choose to save or share them elsewhere.

Contact form

Messages sent through our contact form are delivered to us by Web3Forms. We use them only to reply to you.

Purchase information

Trials, purchases and entitlement checks are handled through Apple. Easy Paint Pro does not receive your full payment-card information.
