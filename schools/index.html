For schools and teachers

A paint program children can read

Every button has its name under it. There’s no account to make and no ads, and pictures stay on the Mac. Students learn it in minutes, and there’s plenty to grow into.

Ask about school licenses Try it free for 7 days

Why it works in a classroom

Easy on the first day. Still useful in June.

Every button says what it does

A picture and a word on every tool, so nobody has to guess what an icon means. Make the toolbar bigger for young eyes, or spread it over two or three rows.

Where: View ▸ Toolbar Size, or the Customize toolbar button at the right end of the toolbar

See how a picture was made

Press Record and the app records just the canvas, never the screen or sound, as a student draws. Replay it at any speed, or export it as a video for a portfolio or a class display.

Where: the Record button above the canvas

Familiar, in the look you choose

A Classic look with tools down the left and colors along the bottom, or a Modern look, each in Day and Night. Pick whichever helps your class.

Where: the Classic Retro Skin button above the canvas, and the Day/Night switch in the toolbar

Lesson ideas

Six projects to start with

Each picture below was made in Easy Paint Pro. Click one to see it full size.

Pixel art, one square at a time

Zoom right in, turn on the grid, and build a character block by block. Great for counting, symmetry and planning.

Tools: Pencil, Fill, Zoom, Grid

A scene from simple shapes

Build a picture from rectangles, circles and polygons, then fill them with gradients, patterns and emoji.

Tools: Shapes, Polygon, Fill with gradient, pattern and emoji

Patterns and repeats

Pick a pattern, change its two colors and its size from 1× to 8×, and talk about what repeats and why.

Tools: Fill with pattern, pattern size

A poster or a title page

Type a headline, give it a 3D or outline look, and lay out shapes around it. Every piece stays editable until it’s placed.

Tools: Text, Text looks, Shapes

Label a diagram

Open a picture of a plant, a map or a machine and label its parts with arrows and text.

Tools: Line with arrows, Text, Open

Explore art materials

Try watercolor, oil paint, crayon, airbrush and calligraphy tips, and compare how each one feels.

Tools: Brush, Airbrush

Safe and simple

What IT and parents want to know

No accounts

Students don’t sign in to anything. There’s nothing to set up and no passwords to manage.

No ads, no tracking

The app shows no advertising and doesn’t track anyone. It’s rated 4+ on the Mac App Store.

Pictures stay on the Mac

Everything is made and saved on the Mac. Nothing is uploaded. Read the privacy policy.

Which Macs

Any Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) running macOS 14 or later. Older Intel Macs can’t run it.
