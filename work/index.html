Point things out in a screenshot

Open a screenshot and add arrows, boxes, circles and text in seconds. Shapes and text stay editable until you place them, so getting it right is easy.

Hide private details before you share

Cover names, email addresses and account numbers with a blur, pixels or a solid block, then export a flattened copy so nobody can undo it.

  • Blur, pixelate or black out any area
  • Export a clean, flattened picture
  • Nothing leaves your Mac

Tools: Privacy cover-ups, Export

Approve, mark paid, file it

Stamp invoices, forms and proofs with Approved, Paid, Confidential, Urgent or your own words, with today’s date. Size it, turn it, place it.

  • Stamp designs sorted into groups, like Classic Stamps
  • Your own wording and the date
  • Place a stamp in front of or behind the artwork

Tools: Stamp

Quick graphics for posts and slides

Make a title card, a banner or a social post with text looks like outline, shadow and 3D, plus shapes, gradients and patterns.

  • Nine text looks, including Block 3D, Gloss 3D and Metal 3D
  • Five kinds of gradient
  • Save as PNG, JPEG, HEIC, WebP, GIF, TIFF, PDF or SVG

Tools: Text, Shapes, Fill

Mark up PDF pages

Open a PDF and each page comes in as its own picture. Draw, stamp or add notes, then save a new PDF to send back.

  • Every page opens in its own tab
  • Stamps, text and drawing on any page
  • Save the marked-up pages as a new PDF

Tools: Open, Stamp, Text, Export as PDF
