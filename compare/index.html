Professional art apps

The big apps do more. They also cost more, often every month.

If you retouch photos for a living or paint with a pressure-sensitive pen all day, a professional app is worth it. For everyday pictures, diagrams, screenshots, school work and fun, Easy Paint Pro is quicker to learn and you pay once.

What you get with Easy Paint Pro

  • A name under every tool, so you never have to guess what an icon does
  • Classic and modern looks, in Day and Night
  • Brushes, patterns, gradients, emoji fills, stamps, text and 3D letters
  • Layers and masks when you want them, out of the way when you don’t
  • Canvas recording, to replay how a picture was made
  • Everything stays on your Mac. No account and no cloud.

Honest answers

When something else is the better choice

Filling in PDF forms

Easy Paint Pro opens PDF pages as pictures to draw on and saves a new PDF. To fill in form fields, sign, or reorder the original pages, use Preview.

Professional photo editing

For camera RAW files, advanced retouching and print color work, a professional photo editor is the right tool.

Logos that scale to any size

Easy Paint Pro paints pixels. For a logo that has to stay sharp at any size, use a vector drawing app.

Windows PCs and iPads

Easy Paint Pro is a Mac app. It needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 14 or later.
