Privacy policy

Short version: your pictures stay on your Mac. We collect only what we need to sell you the app and answer your messages.

This policy covers the Easy Paint Pro app for Mac and the website easypaintpro.com. “We” and “us” mean the maker of Easy Paint Pro.

The app

  • Your artwork stays on your Mac. Pictures, layers, history, recordings, workspaces and exports are created and stored on your device. We never receive them.
  • No account, no tracking. The app has no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics, and it doesn’t track you across apps or websites.
  • Settings. Preferences such as your chosen skin and toolbar layout are stored locally on your Mac.
  • Trial and license. The website version records when your trial started and stores your license key on your Mac. License keys are checked on your Mac; they aren’t sent to us each time you open the app.
  • Updates. The website version checks easypaintpro.com for new versions. That request includes the app version and your macOS version, and like any web request it reveals your IP address to the website host. We don’t use it to identify you.
  • Mac App Store version. When it’s available, purchases and trials will be handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy. We don’t receive your payment details.

Purchases on this website

Orders are processed by our payment partner, which acts as the reseller (merchant of record). It collects your name, email address, billing country and payment details under its own privacy policy, which you’ll see at checkout.

We receive your name, email address, country, order number and the license key issued to you. We use them to deliver your license, provide support, process refunds and keep records required by law. We never receive your full card number.

This website

  • The site doesn’t use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.
  • It’s hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record visitors’ IP addresses for security; see GitHub’s privacy statement.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads; see Google’s privacy policy.

Contact form

Messages you send through our contact form are delivered to us by Web3Forms. They include the name, email address and message you enter, and for license enquiries your organization and number of Macs. We use them only to reply to you and keep them only as long as needed for that conversation.

Your choices

  • Ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, through the contact form. We may need to keep order records where the law requires it.
  • We don’t sell or share your personal information for advertising.
  • Easy Paint Pro is suitable for all ages. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children; purchases are made by an adult through our payment partner.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
